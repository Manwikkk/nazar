## What is Nazar?

Nazar is an AI-powered consumer intelligence platform designed specifically for the Indian market. It continuously analyzes consumer signals from across the web to detect emerging patterns, tensions, and unmet needs before they become obvious.

Instead of waiting for lagging market reports, Nazar converts live consumer conversations into actionable product opportunities for brand builders and venture studios.

---

## Core Features

### 1. Consumer Signal Detection
Nazar constantly monitors and aggregates data from multiple sources—including search trends, social chatter, product reviews, and community discussions—to identify what consumers are actually talking about right now.

### 2. Opportunity Scoring
Every detected signal is processed and ranked. Nazar assigns an **Opportunity Score** based on momentum, price sensitivity, and the severity of consumer complaints, helping you prioritize where to focus your efforts.

### 3. Ask Nazar (AI Research Assistant)
A premium, conversation-first AI workspace. You can ask Nazar direct questions about specific categories, cities, or consumer segments. It acts as your personal market research analyst, drawing on its vast database of signals to give you structured, evidence-backed answers.

### 4. Deep Research & Evidence Tracking
Never rely on gut feeling alone. Every opportunity Nazar presents is backed by raw evidence. You can view the exact consumer quotes, review complaints, and search trends that led to a specific opportunity.

### 5. Regional & Tier-2 Focus
India isn't just one monolithic market. Nazar allows you to filter and explore opportunities specifically gaining traction in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities, where the fastest growth is currently happening.
